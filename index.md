# Free Printables & Classroom Tools Index

A short, hand-picked index of free resources for teachers, parents, homeschoolers and activity leaders. Everything below is free to use and needs no sign-up. Maintained by Vicky at [PrintSheetLab](https://printsheetlab.com).

## Worksheets
- [Free math worksheets with answer keys (K to grade 5)](https://printsheetlab.com/worksheets/): PDFs in US Letter and A4.
- [Math worksheet generator](https://printsheetlab.com/math-worksheet-generator/): make addition, subtraction, multiplication and division sheets with answer keys.
- [Place value chart](https://printsheetlab.com/place-value-chart/)
- [Free homeschool printables](https://printsheetlab.com/free-homeschool-printables/): 100+ worksheets and planners.

## Calculators (with steps)

- [Grade calculator](https://printsheetlab.com/calculators/grade-calculator/): with lookup tables for every score out of 10 to 50.
